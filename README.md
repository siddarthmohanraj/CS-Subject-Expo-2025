# 🏛️ Computer Science Subject Expo 2025

![Reach](https://img.shields.io/badge/Visitors-100%2B_Students-brightgreen?style=for-the-badge)

### What was the event?
Organized and hosted at **The Millennium School Dubai**, the Computer Science Subject Expo was a massive educational exhibition designed to convince younger students to pursue advanced computer science and technology classes. Rather than just standing next to boring poster boards, I completely redesigned how we presented the subject to make it thrilling and interactive.

### How It Worked Simply
*   **Live Interactive Coding:** I set up live computer terminals where students could come up and actually play with real Python code. Instead of talking *at* them using complicated jargon, I showed them visually what the code was doing on the screen in real-time.
*   **Proving Why Speed Matters:** I ran a live race between two different types of search algorithms (the hidden math that finds information). I showed the audience how a basic search took a long time to find a number in a list of 1,000,000 items, while a smart, optimized search found it instantly. It visually proved to non-technical students why writing "good" code is like a superpower.
*   **Changing Minds:** By taking highly complicated mathematical concepts and translating them into fun, easy-to-understand visual games, we completely removed the fear that students had about computer science being "too hard."

### Why It Matters
Leadership isn't just about knowing how to code; it's about knowing how to communicate complex ideas to people who don't understand them. By leading this initiative and speaking passionately to over 100 students and teachers, we massively boosted interest and confidence in the school's computer science programs, inspiring the next generation of student developers.
